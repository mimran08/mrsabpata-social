News-channel style (clocks, red stamps, yellow urgent text).
**Rule:** Clean. Imran's face. Max 4 words. Red #dc2626 accent only.

### ❌ MISTAKE 5: SILENCE
Months of no posts. Algorithm forgot the channel.
**Rule:** Something publishes every day. Never go silent.

### ❌ MISTAKE 6: SUBSCRIBER LOSS IGNORED
Lost 120 subs in 60 days. No emergency action.
**Rule:** Any week with negative net subs triggers content review.

### ❌ MISTAKE 7: WATCH TIME STUCK
More posts, more views, but watch time flat. Lots of bad views < few good views.
**Rule:** Avg view duration is the #1 metric. Never celebrate views without it.

## PART 10: SCRIPT ENGINE — SOLVE THE 22-SECOND PROBLEM

**Hook rule (0-30s):** No cold-open greetings. Title's promise delivered in
sentence 1. Viewer named specifically. Reason to keep watching before 15s.

**Proven hook patterns:**
- Pain callout: "Aap 2 saal se Sweden mein hain. Job hai. Apartment hai. Lekin..."
- Specific promise: "Exactly woh 5 questions jo visa interview mein..."
- Truth reveal: "Sweden visa ke baare mein ek cheez jo koi nahi batata..."
- Story drop: "2019 mein main Stockholm mein ek bench par baitha tha..."

**Structure:** Hook → Context bridge → Body 1 → [retention hook @40%] →
Body 2 → Body 3 → [retention hook @70%] → Body 4 (opt) → CTA + tease
